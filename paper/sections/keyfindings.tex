\begin{keybox}{Key findings}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{2pt}\setlength{\leftmargin}{1em}
  \item \textbf{Training is what matters.} Training with simulated lead failure raises the macro-AUROC of the
        \emph{same} ResNet by \gainDropKTen{} when ten of twelve leads are missing, at no cost on complete ECGs.
  \item \textbf{Quality gating adds nothing measurable.} Gating attention by a learned per-lead quality score does
        not beat un-gated lead attention, and the full system is never significantly better than a
        corruption-trained ResNet (worse in \bootNSigNeg{} of \bootNRows{} comparisons).
  \item \textbf{An illusion of certainty.} As leads disappear, accuracy falls (macro-AUROC \aucLgEdlKZero{}
        $\rightarrow$ \aucLgEdlKTen) while the evidential uncertainty does not rise (\meanUKZero{} $\rightarrow$
        \meanUKTen). The model loses accuracy, not confidence; abstention came only from counting usable leads.
\end{itemize}
\end{keybox}
