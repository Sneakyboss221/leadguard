\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\subsection{Training}
\label{sec:setup-train}
All neural models share one training recipe without per-model tuning: AdamW (learning rate $10^{-3}$, weight decay
$10^{-2}$), cosine learning-rate decay over all steps, batches of 128, at most 15 epochs, and early stopping with a
patience of four epochs on the macro-AUROC of the clean validation fold, whose best checkpoint is evaluated.
Selecting on clean data keeps checkpoint selection independent of the test corruptions but may favour clean
performance over robustness. Mixed precision is used on the GPU. Corruption-trained models corrupt every batch on the
fly (Section~\ref{sec:data-train}), and the quality-aware models receive $\mathbf{q}$ from the frozen assessor
applied to that corrupted batch. LG-EDL is trained with Eq.~\eqref{eq:edlloss} and the other neural models with
binary cross-entropy. LG-EDL and the corruption-trained ResNet, the two models compared directly, were trained with
three seeds (0, 1, 2); every other variant was trained once. The 13 trainings (twelve neural models and XGBoost) took
\gpuMinutes{} minutes of wall-clock time on one GPU.

\subsection{Post-Hoc Fitting on the Validation Fold}
\label{sec:setup-fit}
Three quantities are fitted on clean validation predictions only. (i) Every model except LG-EDL receives one
temperature $T$, applied as $\sigma(\mathrm{logit}(p)/T)$ and chosen to minimise the binary cross-entropy over the
five classes ($T\in[e^{-3},e^{3}]$); LG-EDL's probabilities are used as the evidential head produces them. This gives
the sigmoid models a calibration step that LG-EDL does not receive, which favours them in calibration comparisons.
(ii) Each model receives a decision threshold per class that maximises that class's F1 score on a grid from 0.01 to
0.99. (iii) LG-EDL receives the abstention threshold $\tau$ of Section~\ref{sec:method-abstain}.

\subsection{Metrics}
\label{sec:setup-metrics}
The primary metric is macro-AUROC over the five classes, which does not depend on thresholds. We also report macro-F1
at the tuned thresholds, per-class AUROC, and the expected calibration error (ECE) over all $N\times5$ binary
predictions in 15 equal-width bins. Because most of these predictions are negatives of low-prevalence classes, this
pooled ECE is dominated by small probabilities.

For selective prediction, the risk of a record is the fraction of its five labels predicted wrongly at the tuned
thresholds. Records are sorted from most to least certain by an uncertainty score, and the mean risk of the kept
records is reported at 20 coverage levels from 5\% to 100\%. LG-EDL is ranked by its evidential uncertainty $U$ and,
for comparison, by the probability-based score $s_p=1-\frac{1}{5}\sum_c\max(p_c,1-p_c)$, which is used for every
other model. Higher values of $U$ and $s_p$ mean lower certainty.

LG-EDL and the corruption-trained ResNet are compared with a paired bootstrap over test records: in each of
\bootNBoot{} resamples, both models' macro-AUROC are computed on the same records, each averaged over its three
seeds, and the 95\% interval of the difference is taken from the percentiles. The intervals reflect test-set
sampling, not training variability, which is reported separately as the standard deviation over seeds. They are
given per condition without correction for multiple comparisons. The single-seed ablation variants are compared
descriptively.

To separate the value of gating from the accuracy of the quality estimate, the gated models are also evaluated with
the true reliability $q^\ast_j=1-s_j$ in place of the assessor's estimate (oracle control). The assessor's own
accuracy is measured as the agreement of $\mathbf{1}[q_j\ge0.5]$ with the reliable label $\mathbf{1}[s_j<0.5]$ on
corrupted copies of the test fold, over all leads and excluding the borderline band $|s_j-0.5|<0.1$. Excluding the
band, the lowest agreement over failure types is \sqaGate{} (\sqaGateType); including it, EMG at the test SNR, whose
severity lies exactly on the boundary, is matched on \sqaEmgTestCorrupted{} of its corrupted leads.

\subsection{Reproducibility}
\label{sec:setup-repro}
Training runs can be resumed from checkpoints that store all random-number states, and the test-time corruption is
deterministic. Twenty unit tests, including the exact invariance of the masked models to replaced leads, attention
rows that sum to one with zeros on masked keys, deterministic test corruption and an overfitting check, passed
before training. The tables and reported numbers are generated by a script from the released result files.
